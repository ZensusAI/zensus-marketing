# Comparison substantiation: Cash Flow Frog, October 5, 2026

Evidence file for `/compare/zensus-vs-cash-flow-frog`, which is driven by
`CASH_FLOW_FROG_COMPARE` in `src/lib/compare-pages.ts`. It adds to
`comparison-substantiation-2026-09-21.md`, which still covers the homepage
matrix and the other three comparison pages, and it follows the same house
rules (FTC comparative advertising, Lanham Act substantiation, nominative fair
use).

Every Cash Flow Frog fact below was read from Cash Flow Frog's own public
pages on 2026-10-05, and the quoted words appear on the page named. Nothing
here comes from review sites, search snippets or memory. "Not found" means
not found on Cash Flow Frog's public pages on that date. It is never a claim
that the capability does not exist.

Re-verify with the quarterly review (next: December 2026), or sooner if Cash
Flow Frog changes its pricing page. Its "Early bird" yearly rate has no stated
end date, so that figure is the most likely to move.

## How the prices were read

The pricing page opens on the **Yearly** tab. A plain fetch of the page
therefore shows yearly prices only. The monthly prices below were read in a
browser with the **Monthly** tab selected and the revenue slider moved through
each tier. The page also has a **2 Years** tab, which the comparison does not
use.

## Pricing

Source: https://cashflowfrog.com/pricing/

- One plan, "Pro Plan", in five revenue tiers: "Your price is set
  automatically based on your annual revenue."
- Currency and card: "No credit card required • Cancel anytime • USD"
- Every tier lists: "Cash flow forecasting up to 36 months", "Scenario
  planning", "Up to 10 users".
- Also on the page: "30-day money-back guarantee".

| Tier | Revenue band, as labelled | Monthly | Yearly, list | Yearly, "Early bird" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Up to $1M annual revenue" | $69.00 | $55.00 | $33.00 |
| 2 | $1M to $5M | $89.00 | $71.00 | $43.00 |
| 3 | $5M to $10M | $129.00 | $103.00 | $62.00 |
| 4 | $10M to $20M | $179.00 | $143.00 | $86.00 |
| 5 | "$20M+ annual revenue" | $249.00 | $199.00 | $119.00 |

On the Yearly tab the first tier renders as "$55.00/mo" with "20% yearly",
then "$33.00 /mo" with "Early bird 40%". The Monthly tab shows "$69.00 /mo"
with no early-bird label. No end date or eligibility rule for "Early bird" was
found on the page.

What the comparison says, and why it is supportable:

- "Cheaper at every tier below $20M": monthly prices of $69, $89, $129 and
  $179 are each below Zensus Pro's $199.
- Above $20M: $249 monthly is above $199; $199 on yearly billing equals it.
  The page states both and does not claim Zensus is cheaper.

## Free trial

  required · Full access from day one"

## Data connections

- Accounting systems (https://cashflowfrog.com/ai/): "QuickBooks Online,
  QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, Sage Intacct, Odoo, Zoho Books, FreshBooks".
- Bank feed (https://cashflowfrog.com/integrations/plaid/): "Cash Flow Frog's
  Plaid connection currently supports banks in the US and Canada, covering
  thousands of financial institutions including Chase, Bank of America, Wells
  Fargo, Citi, and more."
- Spreadsheets (https://cashflowfrog.com/integrations/): "import bank
  transactions from an Excel or CSV file".
- CRM. Two of its pages read differently, so the comparison uses wording both
  support: no CRM connection of its own, with deals pushed in through Zapier.
  - https://cashflowfrog.com/ai/: "There are no opportunity stages, no quotas,
    no rep performance tracking, and no CRM connection."
  - https://cashflowfrog.com/integrations/plaid/: "HubSpot or Salesforce, Add
    expected deal closings as future cash inflows." and "Cash Flow Frog
    supports Zapier, so you can push projected revenue and expenses from your
    CRM, invoicing platform, project management tool, or accounting software
    directly into your forecast."

## What the page says about Zensus

Each statement about Zensus on the comparison page was checked against the
app's own code in October 2026: QuickBooks Online is the only accounting
connection; HubSpot means invoices and subscriptions, not deals; the default
projection runs 36 months; the forecast uses one currency and converts
nothing; there is no spreadsheet import; and Slack is the only alert channel.
