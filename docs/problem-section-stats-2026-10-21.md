# Homepage problem section: statistics and their sources

Read on 21 October 2026, for the four stat columns in `src/components/landing/Problem.tsx` (homepage redesign handoff 06-problem). Quotes are verbatim from each source page. Re-read a source before changing its figure or its wording on the site.

## 39%: one late payment made payroll or bills hard to cover

- Source: QuickBooks, "2026 Small Business Late Payments Report"
- URL: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/small-business-data/small-business-late-payments-report-2026/
- Quote: "Nearly 2 in 5 owners (39%) say one late payment made it hard to cover payroll or bills in the past year."
- Date: the page's "Published on" field is blank; its metadata carries 2026-07-07 and 2026-07-09.

## 61%: data reliability is the main hurdle

- Source: AFP, "Benchmarks for Financial Professionals: 20 Key Stats on Salaries, Fraud and Tech" (12/17/2025), citing the 2025 AFP FP&A Benchmarking Survey
- URL: https://www.financialprofessionals.org/training-resources/resources/articles/Details/benchmarks-for-financial-professionals-20-key-stats-on-salaries-fraud-and-tech
- Quote: "The survey points to a foundational issue: 61% of FP&A professionals cite lack of data reliability as their primary hurdle."

## 96%: spreadsheets are still used for planning

- Source: the same AFP article. It does not name the survey behind this figure, so the site cites the article, not the benchmarking survey.
- Quote: "Spreadsheets are still used by 96% of FP&A professionals for planning."
- Correction: the handoff said "still plan in spreadsheets at least weekly". The source says nothing about frequency, so the site says "still use spreadsheets for planning".

## 52%: revenue forecasts miss actuals by more than 6%

- Source: Vena, "7 FP&A Trends Shaping 2026" (June 4, 2026), citing Vena's 2026 FP&A Impact Report (survey of over 400 finance leaders)
- URL: https://www.venasolutions.com/blog/fpa-trends-predictions
- Quote: "Fifty-two percent of finance leaders say their revenue forecasts differ from actual results by more than 6%."
